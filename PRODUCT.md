# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Homeowners and business owners in the North Carolina Triad area (including Greensboro, Winston-Salem, High Point, and Burlington) who need septic installations, repairs, pumping, maintenance, or commercial grease trap cleaning. They may be in high-stress emergency situations (active septic failure) or looking for a reliable, long-term maintenance partner.

## Product Purpose
To attract local residential and commercial leads, establish immediate professional credibility, verify active licensing/insurance, and drive phone calls and service requests.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, local, expert.

## Anti-references
- SaaS-style generic landing pages with hero-metrics and tech grids.
- Low-quality local directories or spammy construction portals.
- Over-animated, distracting layouts that feel sales-pitchy rather than service-oriented.

## Design Principles
- **Local Trust Indicators**: Focus on verified badges, licensing, local service area listings, and reviews.
- **Immediate Assistance Accessibility**: Make contact options (specifically click-to-call) extremely prominent and easy to locate under stress.
- **Clarity Over Novelty**: Keep navigation and layout highly readable and structured to respect the user's immediate need.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Legible typography optimized for all age groups (min 16px body, high weight contrast).
- WCAG AA compliant color contrast ratios.
- Responsive tap targets for quick tap-to-call on mobile.
